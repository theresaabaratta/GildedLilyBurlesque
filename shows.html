The Crown Jewel · Annual Valentine's Spectacular

Tassels & Champagne

Since 2011, every February has belonged to Gilded Lily. "Tassels & Champagne" is Baltimore's most beloved burlesque tradition — a Valentine's Day spectacular at Creative Alliance that has welcomed national headliners, local legends, live music, champagne cocktail hours, and audiences dressed to the nines for 14 consecutive years.

T&C
Tassels & Champagne
Annual Valentine's Burlesque & Variety Show · Creative Alliance · Baltimore

What started as a Valentine's Day showcase has evolved into Gilded Lily's most ambitious annual production. Each edition carries a distinct theme — from heartfelt Baltimore tributes to bold celebrations of queer love — and draws performers from across the East Coast and beyond.

The 14th annual edition in 2025 opened with a pre-show cocktail & mocktail hour hosted by "Baltimore's reigning Mistress of Smut" Jocelyn Loverling, followed by an evening of burlesque, aerial arts, and variety from performers including Miss Exotic World 2023 Samson Night and GiGi Holliday — ranked No. 10 Most Influential Burlesque Performer in the World.

What to Expect

✦ Pre-show cocktail & mocktail hour with entertainment
✦ National & local guest headliners (NYC, Philly, DC & Baltimore)
✦ Retro glamour atmosphere & champagne table packages
✦ Live streaming option for stay-at-home audiences
✦ Variety acts: aerialists, belly dancers, drag, boylesque

Show Catalogue

From pop culture tributes to political cabaret, live music spectacles to surrealist variety — every Gilded Lily show was its own world.

Pop Culture Tribute
Taran-Tease
A Burlesque Tribute to Quentin Tarantino

Feb 23, 2013 · The Windup Space

Have you wondered what was in Samuel L. Jackson's briefcase in Pulp Fiction? Were you seduced by Salma Hayek's snake dance in From Dusk Till Dawn? Did Uma Thurman's deadly moves make you want to join the Deadly Viper Assassination Squad? In celebration of Django Unchained, this twisted cast of titillating talent paid tribute to the cult director of the decade. One of Gilded Lily's most iconic and talked-about themed productions.

Cast: Maria Bella · Sophia Sunday · GiGi Holliday · Roma Mafia · Nona Narcisse (New Orleans) · Creamy Stevens (NYC) · MC: Swami Yomahmi · Stage Mgr: Lauren Marleaux

📍The Windup Space, 12 W. North Ave, Station North
🎬Inspired by Django Unchained, Pulp Fiction, Kill Bill, From Dusk Till Dawn
Avant-Garde
Surrealesque
A Surrealist Burlesque Experience

Summer 2012 · Creative Alliance

One of the troupe's most daring early experiments, Surrealesque pushed Gilded Lily's theatrical instincts beyond classic striptease into something more dreamlike and disorienting — in the most delicious way. Featuring the return of Armitage Shanks, whose wild presence elevated the show into genuine performance art territory. Baltimore Magazine later cited this as a defining moment in the troupe's evolution.

Cast: Maria Bella · Armitage Shanks · Gilded Lily company

📍Creative Alliance, Patterson Theater, Highlandtown
🎨Mixed medium performance art + classic burlesque
Live Music
Instrumental Tease
Burlesque + Live Blues & Swing

Multiple Dates · The Windup Space

An evening with the best burlesque, variety, and live music the local Baltimore scene has to offer. Performers tease to the raw, in-the-room sound of live bands — transporting the audience to a bygone era of supper clubs and smoke-filled jazz lounges. The first edition featured Maria Bella, Miss Ruby Sparkles, and Lauren Marleaux performing to live blues and mountain swing. Later editions brought Sean K. Preston & the Loaded Pistols, and Jonny Grave & the Tombstones.

Cast: Maria Bella · Lauren Marleaux · Miss Ruby Sparkles · and rotating live bands

📍The Windup Space, Station North
🎺Live blues, mountain swing, rock & roll accompaniment
🎸Bands: Sean K. Preston & the Loaded Pistols · Jonny Grave & the Tombstones
Sideshow & Variety
The Carnivalesque
A Burlesque Carnival Spectacular

2013 · Multiple Venues

"There will be a midway, with acrobats, barkers, wayward women, a freak show — just about everything you'd see at a Coney Island carnival. There will be games, too." Maria Bella described this as "like no other show she has ever produced." Featuring sideshow art and oddities from the collection of Shocked and Amazed author James Taylor, and a full cast of circus performers, vaudeville entertainers, and burlesque stars.

Cast: Maria Bella · Armitage Shanks · GiGi Holliday · Kay Sera · Mourna Handful · Mab Just Mab · Patsi Cake (trapeze) · Sophia Sunday · Jonathan Burns (vaudeville contortionist/magician) · Russell Bruner (King of Boylesque 2012)

🎠Full Coney Island-style midway experience
🃏Sideshow art from the James Taylor "Shocked and Amazed" collection
Activism & Politics
Fight the Power
A Political Burlesque Cabaret

2016 · Creative Alliance

Inspired by the Baltimore Uprising and the 2016 election, this Creative Alliance show tackled topical issues through both playful and poignant acts. A portion of the proceeds went to the criminal justice reform nonprofit the Innocence Project. The evening's most powerful moment: GiGi Holliday performed to Michael Jackson's "They Don't Care About Us," wearing a white bodysuit that read "Say Their Name." She ran offstage and cried into Maria's arms. "Burlesque is my own protest," GiGi said. Guest performer Betty O'Hellno dressed as a mermaidian Statue of Liberty to Lana Del Rey.

Cast: GiGi Holliday · Maria Bella · Betty O'Hellno · Full Gilded Lily company

✊Proceeds benefited the Innocence Project
📍Creative Alliance, Patterson Theater
🎵Featured: "They Don't Care About Us" — Michael Jackson
Touring Production
Return to Glamour
Gilded Lily Returns to Washington DC

2019 · Dance Place · Washington DC

"The sparkle of a rhinestone in the spotlight. The hypnotic shimmy of fringe across the hips. The caress of a silk stocking along the leg." Return to Glamour marked Gilded Lily's long-awaited return to the nation's capital, performed at the prestigious Dance Place. A celebration of returns — live burlesque, glitz, glamour — with live music from Jenny Langer & the Moonshine Society and a cast spanning Philadelphia, DC, and Baltimore.

Cast: Maria Bella · Eden · Delilah Dentata · Maria TopCatt (Philly host) · SirVixen · Bunny Vicious · Thandiwe Zulu

📍Dance Place · Washington DC
🎵Live music: Jenny Langer & the Moonshine Society
Gallery & Stage
Workin' the Tease
The Art of Baltimore Burlesque · MICA Exhibition

2014 · The Lyric Opera House · MICA

In 2014, the Maryland Institute College of Art celebrated Baltimore's burlesque scene with a landmark exhibition — "Workin' the Tease" — featuring portraits, costumes, and an entire wall of pasties in the hallowed halls of The Lyric Opera House. Gilded Lily was a featured participant, with Maria Bella helping develop the exhibition seminar. "It was like A League of Their Own," Maria later said. "It was wonderful. We all cried being up on that stage for the first time."

Featured: Maria Bella · The full Baltimore burlesque community · Exhibition curated through MICA

📍The Lyric Opera House · Maryland Institute College of Art
🏛️Exhibitions: portraits, costumes, and a full wall of pasties
Touring Production
The Carnivalesque Roadshow
East Coast Touring Variety Show

Multiple Years · East Coast

Maria Bella's touring production co-created with Armitage Shanks took the Gilded Lily spirit on the road up and down the East Coast — blending burlesque, sideshow, circus arts, and variety performance. The Carnivalesque Roadshow was featured at the Festival of Doom in Washington DC and at the Asheville Americana Burlesque & Sideshow Festival, and helped establish Maria Bella as a nationally touring headliner.

Co-produced by: Maria Bella & Armitage Shanks

🗺️East Coast touring — Baltimore, DC, Asheville & more
🎪Featured at: Festival of Doom (DC) · ABSFest (Asheville)

Burlesque, Brunch & Live Music

May 17, 2015 · The Windup Space

A collaboration between Gilded Lily Burlesque and House of Sweetbottom that combined Sunday brunch, live music from Jonny Grave & the Tombstones, and full burlesque performance — one of the more unusual and beloved one-off concepts the troupe ever produced. Gilded Lily Co-Produces

Charm City Burlesque & Variety Festival

For years, Maria Bella dreamed of a Baltimore burlesque festival — and in 2023, she made it real. The inaugural Charm City Burlesque & Variety Festival (CCBVF) ran September 29–October 1 at Creative Alliance, co-produced by Maria Bella, Tapitha Kix, and Bella La Blanc.

"We need to," Maria had told Baltimore Magazine years earlier. "And it's going to be all-embracing to showcase all of the talents of our city. It's going to show that we're here. And we're not going anywhere."

The festival combined burlesque, drag, sideshow, circus, belly dance, and other variety arts — celebrating Baltimore's full creative spectrum across three nights of programming. "Our festival combines burlesque, drag, sideshow, circus, belly dance and other variety arts," said Maria. "Burlesque means to turn things on its head."

3 Nights Creative Alliance Annual · Fall BIPOC & LGBTQ+ Centered DMV Community

Beyond Baltimore

Touring & Special Appearances

Gilded Lily has always been Baltimore first — but the spotlight has a way of expanding. Here are some of the troupe's most notable out-of-town and special event appearances.

🗽
New York Burlesque Festival
New York City · 2012 & 2013

Maria Bella earned silver at the NYC Burlesque Festival star search at The Ottobar, earning a full invitation to perform alongside 150 national acts at the New York Burlesque Festival. She returned as a featured artist the following year.

🍁
Toronto Burlesque Festival
Toronto, Canada · Featured Artist

Maria Bella was selected as a featured artist at one of North America's most prestigious burlesque festivals, taking Gilded Lily's Baltimore spirit across the border for an international audience.

🇬🇧
London Burlesque Festival
London, United Kingdom · 2014

One of the most prestigious burlesque festivals in the world — and Maria Bella was selected to perform on its stage in 2014, making Gilded Lily an internationally recognized name in neo-burlesque.

🏔️
Asheville Americana Burlesque & Sideshow Festival
Asheville, NC · Headliner & Artist of the Year 2016

Maria Bella was named Artist of the Year by ABSFest in 2016 — and also served as the festival's headliner that same year. A career high-water mark for Gilded Lily's founder.

🎭
Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts
Washington, DC · Opening Act

Maria Bella opened for Storm Large of Pink Martini at the Kennedy Center — perhaps the most prestigious single venue appearance in Gilded Lily history, bringing burlesque to one of America's great concert halls.

🌵
Texas Burlesque Festival
Texas

Maria Bella took Gilded Lily's signature blend of classic cabaret and neo-burlesque to Texas, continuing to build the troupe's national profile through festival touring.

🌹
Southern Fried Burlesque Festival
Atlanta, GA · Queen Category Finalist

Competed and performed in the Queen category at one of the South's most celebrated burlesque festivals — another milestone in Maria Bella's decade-spanning festival touring career.

🦅
Dance Place
Washington, DC · 2019 Return to Glamour

Gilded Lily's full company debut at Dance Place DC, a respected performing arts center rooted in movement arts. The show — Return to Glamour — featured live music and a multi-city cast, and was described by Dance Place as "a celebration of returns."

What It's Like to Be There

The Gilded Lily Experience

Every show is its own world. But some things are always true about a night with Gilded Lily.

🍾
Arrive Early

Pre-show programming — cocktail hours, interactive games, vendor markets, and warm-up entertainment — are part of the Gilded Lily tradition. Get there at doors to get the full experience.

👗
Dress the Part

Audiences are encouraged to don their finest frocks and dapper duds. Retro glamour, vintage flair, and creative costuming are all celebrated — the show doesn't end at the stage.

📣
Cheer Out Loud

"Laugh out loud, cheer, hoot and holler" — this isn't a quiet concert. Gilded Lily audiences participate. Your energy feeds the performers. The room becomes part of the show.

🤝
Respect the Stage

Consent always matters. Always obtain permission before interacting with performers. Gilded Lily shows are a space of celebration, but also of respect — for the artists and for each other.
